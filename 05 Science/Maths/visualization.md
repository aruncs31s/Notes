This means cosine is the **average** of two counter-rotating phasors $e^{ix}$ (anti-clockwise) and $e^{-ix}$ (clockwise) in the complex plane. Their imaginary parts cancel, leaving only the real projection — which is $\cos x$.

Geometrically: two unit vectors rotating in opposite directions, summed and halved, trace a pure oscillation on the real axis.
